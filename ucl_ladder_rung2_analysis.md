## 4. Risk Flag: Both Legs Share One Failure Mode

Arsenal and Liverpool **both played Champions League matches on Wednesday** and both turn
around in three days. The two matches are statistically independent, but the parlay is doubly
exposed to the same *type* of risk — post-European rotation, fatigue, and a manager resting
starters ahead of the next European round.

This is the structural weakness of this rung and it is not reflected in either price.
