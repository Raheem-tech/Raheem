## The Honest Counter-Case

I would not present this as a strong pick without the following:

**1. Head-to-head is bad.** Atlético have won **7 of the last 9** meetings. Sevilla's saving grace: they won the most recent one, **2-1 in April 2026**.

**2. Sevilla's 2025-26 baseline was poor.** Through 30 league games last season they were **W8 D7 L15, 37 scored / 50 conceded**, with only **two home clean sheets**, **1.6 goals conceded per home game**, and roughly **one point per game at home**. Almeyda was sacked on 23 March and replaced by Luis García. Two good weeks do not erase that.

**3. The sample is two matches, and one is flattered.** Athletic Club played from the 11th minute with ten men (Yuri Berchiche red card) in Sevilla's 3-1 at San Mamés. That result is real, but it is not evidence that Sevilla can beat a full-strength top-four side.

**4. Squad churn.** Sevilla lost Azpilicueta, Alexis Sánchez, Gudelj, Januzaj, Kouassi, Juanlu Sánchez, Sow and Adams over the summer, bringing in Guridi and Sangante. Early-season cohesion in a rebuilt squad is fragile.

**5. Simeone's floor is high even when depleted.** An Atlético side without strikers does not become bad — it becomes *cagey*. A 1-0 away win off a set piece is entirely within their range, and it is the exact scenario that beats this bet.

**6. The Álvarez risk is live.** If the transfer saga resolves in the next few hours and he starts, the core of the thesis weakens materially. **Check confirmed lineups approximately one hour before the 19:30 UTC kickoff.** If Álvarez is in the XI, this pick drops from moderate–high to a pass.

## Data Integrity — What I Could and Could Not Verify

**Access constraint:** All direct page fetching was blocked in this environment. FBref, ESPN, Wikipedia, premierleague.com, LiveSoccerTV, Sofascore and football-data.org all returned egress-proxy 403s to both `WebFetch` and `curl`. **Every figure above comes from web-search result summaries, not from primary statistical tables.** Anyone acting on this should re-pull the underlying numbers from FBref, Understat, or the official league sites.

**Specific reliability flags:**

- **No xG anywhere.** I could not obtain a single expected-goals figure for either side this season. That is the biggest gap in this analysis. Sevilla's 6-from-6 could be materially over- or under-performing its underlying numbers and I have no way to tell.
- **Search summaries mixed seasons and competitions.** One Premier League fixture list repeatedly returned "Coventry City v Hull City" as a top-flight match — that is a Championship tie, and its presence proves the summarising layer was blending sources. Treat all fixture lists here as directional.
- **Conflicting La Liga MD3 listings.** One query returned "Levante v Real Betis," another "Levante v Real Madrid." The latter is consistent with Real Madrid having beaten Real Sociedad 4-1 in MD2 and is the version used above.

The away figures are directionally sound and consistent with the qualitative reporting, but the exact numbers should be re-verified.
- **Hjulmand's status is contradicted across sources** — listed as unavailable in one preview, described as training normally in another.
- **Odds are unstable and book-dependent.** Atlético appeared at both 2.06 (-103) and 2.20 across sources; the draw price was never confirmed at all, so the ~1.80–1.90 Double Chance estimate is derived, not quoted. **Price it at your own book before staking.**
